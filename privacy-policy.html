Downtime — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Downtime collects no user data.

No data collection

The app runs entirely offline and makes no network connections of any kind. No personal information, usage data, analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports, or any other data is collected, stored, transmitted, or shared.

No third-party services

Downtime does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising networks, or cloud services.

Local storage

Game progress (high scores, completed levels) is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app.
