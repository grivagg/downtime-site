Downtime - Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Downtime collects no personal data about you.

No data collection

No personal information, usage data, analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports, or any other data about you or your device is collected, stored, transmitted, or shared. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no profile associated with you.

No third-party services

Downtime does not use any analytics libraries, advertising networks, attribution or tracking SDKs, or cloud services. There are no adverts in the app.

Playing is always offline

Every game plays fully offline. Put your device in airplane mode and nothing changes - no sign-in, no loading screen, and nothing waiting on a server. Playing Downtime never requires a network connection, and the app does not send your game data anywhere.

Local storage

Game progress - high scores, completed levels, objectives, medals, and your settings - is saved locally on your device only. It never leaves your device, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

Downtime does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected at all, there is nothing to request access to, correct, or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
