Downtime - Press Kit

Last updated: July 2026

Downtime is a small collection of offline puzzle games for Android, built for the moments in between - a flight, a commute, a queue, a waiting room. Every game plays fully offline: put the phone in airplane mode and nothing changes. There are no adverts, no account, no sign-in and no data collected about the player. Both games are complete, and free.

It exists because the "offline games" category on Android is mostly ad-funded collections that need a connection to show you the ads. Downtime is the opposite of that: two finished games, about 7 MB, that work at 35,000 feet. The whole app was built by a solo developer working with a team of AI coding agents.

The two games

Tetrix - a fast, clean block puzzle. Stack the falling pieces, clear the lines, and see how deep you can get before the speed catches up with you. A good run takes 5–20 minutes, which is about the length of a taxi ride to the gate.

Carry On - 99 box-pushing puzzles in the classic sokoban style. The goal is simple: push every box onto its mark. Most of the levels come from Microban, a well-known set by David W. Skinner, used here with credit. The early ones take seconds; the later ones will follow you around for the rest of the day.

Each game has objectives to chase and a bronze, silver or gold medal to earn. Progress saves itself - close the app mid-level, come back three days later, and it is exactly where you left it.
