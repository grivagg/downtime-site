Does Downtime need the internet? Can I play in airplane mode?

You don't need internet and can play in airplane mode. This is the main idea of Downtime - you can play during a flight, or on your daily commute on the underground. Downtime itself does not need the internet; you might only need it if you want to rate us on the Play Store or read this FAQ on playdowntime.com.

What permissions does Downtime need, and why?

Until version 1.4.0, Downtime needed no permissions at all. It now needs them only for these features:

  • Reminders about the daily puzzles. These are off by default, and you will be asked for permission only when you turn the reminder on. You can turn notifications off again at any time, in Downtime or in your phone's settings. The reminder also uses the "Run at startup" permission, which lets the reminder come back if you restart your phone. With the reminder off, it does nothing.
  • Rating us on the Play Store. We might ask you to rate the game on the Play Store. We only ask when you have an internet connection, and to know that, the app uses the "View network connections" permission. It's a low-risk, read-only permission that Android grants automatically. It can only see whether you're online, never what you do online.

None of these lets Downtime send anything anywhere. Downtime has no internet permission.

Can Downtime remind me about the daily puzzles?

Yes, if you want it to. The reminder is off by default. Turn it on in Settings, choose a time, and allow notifications when your phone asks. You'll get one short note a day, and only if you haven't completed the daily tasks yet.

What data do you collect about me? Is it safe?

We collect nothing and know nothing about you. There is no account, no email and no sign-up. Your progress, settings and daily results stay on your phone (however, remember that Google backs this data up to your Google cloud). Uninstalling Downtime deletes it from your phone.

Any online feature we add in the future, such as a leaderboard, will always be optional and off by default. Downtime will never share anything you haven't clearly allowed.

How much of my battery does Downtime use?

Downtime is made for playing on the move: on a flight or a long commute, often far from a charger. So we specifically developed, optimised and measured every game to be light on battery.

We compared each game with the phone's empty home screen on. All games add only about 5–20% on top of that normal screen-on use. Most of the power any game uses goes to the screen itself.

How much memory does Downtime use?

Downtime itself uses about 60–140 MB while you play, depending on the game.

Something looks wrong on the screen. What can I do?

Try turning off Settings › Advanced › Use graphics chip; some phones draw better without it. Downtime also turns it off automatically if it notices drawing problems. Either way, please let us know at the developer contact email at the bottom of this page. If it's a problem in the game, we'd like to fix it.

Does Downtime show ads?

No. We don't show ads, and Downtime doesn't even have an internet connection to get them. The games are designed to be played with as few interruptions as possible, and ads don't fit that idea.

Does the daily puzzle need the internet?

No. Each day's puzzle is defined by the date, so everyone gets the same puzzle without the app going online. New dailies start at midnight GMT, the same time and the same puzzle for all users.

You can share your result through Android's standard share menu; sending it might need an internet connection, depending on the app you share to. We also plan to add a leaderboard in a later release. It will be off by default, and you'll need to turn it on yourself.

Will I lose my game if I close the app?

No. Every game saves as you play. You can close the app mid-level, restart your phone, get back to the game days later, and it will continue exactly where you left it.

Can I play with a gamepad?

Yes, Downtime has full gamepad support.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

We don't collect your data, and we don't save your progress anywhere. All your progress and medals are on your phone only, and in your phone's Android backup.
