Downtime

Offline puzzle games for flights and commutes.

Two complete puzzle games for Android that work with no internet at all. Put your phone in airplane mode and nothing changes. No adverts, no account, no sign-in, and no data collected about you. About 7 MB.

Coming soon to Google Play.

The games

Tetrix - a fast, clean block puzzle. Stack the falling pieces, clear the lines, and see how deep you can get before the speed catches up with you.

Carry On - 99 box-pushing puzzles in the classic sokoban style, most from David W. Skinner's Microban set. Push every box onto its mark.
